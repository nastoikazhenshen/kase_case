# KASE BondScope — visual basis

The interface uses the linked KASE corporate guide (2016) as a visual reference: Graphite `#45464F`, Green 1 `#038726`, Green 2 `#3CBD0D`, Green 3 `#9ED110`, Yellow `#FECC00`, Orange `#EF7F1A`, and Blue `#0094DE`. Source: [KASE corporate style](https://kase.kz/ru/media-center/corporate-style) and the [linked guide PDF](https://kase.kz/uploads/Pravila_KASE_logo_82222dd79b.pdf), p. 9, §1.8.

The prototype uses a white/light operational workspace, graphite text, Arial or a system sans-serif, compact tables, and dark KASE green for navigation and actions. Green describes an interface action, never investment safety. The original logo and font files are not copied or embedded; the product is text-identified as a competition prototype and does not imply KASE endorsement.

Status labels always include words and are not communicated by colour alone. Tables remain scrollable at narrow widths, filters are native keyboard-operable controls, and external sources are exposed as HTTP(S) links after validation.
